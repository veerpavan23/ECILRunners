The Mission

Who are ECIL Runners?

Based at the ECIL Officers Association (ECOA), ECIL Runners is a vibrant and inclusive family built around a shared passion for fitness, health, and friendship. We believe that running is not just about the kilometers you cover�it's about the people you meet, the challenges you overcome, and the person you become along the way.

Everyone starts somewhere. You don't have to be an experienced runner to be part of our family. Whether you are lacing up your shoes for the very first time to embrace a healthier lifestyle, or you are a seasoned athlete training for an 80K trail run, you belong here. Our community is built on encouragement, consistency, and mutual support because every runner's goal deserves to be celebrated.

Serving ECIL, Moula Ali, Sainikpuri, Marredpally, and surrounding neighborhoods, our top priority is a safe, positive, and respectful environment. From your first run to your first Ultra: Run Together. Grow Together. Go Beyond.
